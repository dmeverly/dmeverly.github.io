Useful Tools

A few handy tools I've designed for personal use that may be useful to others.

Quick QR Code Generator

Type a web address or any text and get a QR code that phones can scan with their camera. The code updates as you type. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

0 of 500 characters

PNG works almost everywhere. SVG stays sharp at any size, which is best for printing. Test the code with your phone before you share it.

Your QR code will appear here as you type.

Templated Schedule Mapper

Do you write employee schedules from a repeating template, then retype the names into a calendar every month? This tool does that part for you. Give it your rotation template and it builds a full 12-month calendar, plus a separate calendar for each employee, in seconds.

Your file stays on your device. Everything is processed in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

  1. 1Add your template
  2. 2Name your shifts
  3. 3Pick a start date
  4. 4Download

1 Add your template

Don't have a template? See an example or get a blank one

Days of the week run across the top and each row is a week of your rotation. Type names under the days people work. The top-left cell names the shift. For more shifts, repeat the block to the right.

Example: a two-week rotation with one shift, "Day"
Day Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat Sun
1 Alex Alex Alex Sam Sam
2 Sam Sam Alex Alex Alex Alex

2 Name your shifts

Name each shift, then pick its color. The name appears next to each person on the calendar, like "Alex - Day".
